Hi there! 

I am now a research associate in geometric generative models applied to molecular data, working with [Michael Bronstein](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/michael.bronstein/) in Oxford.

Before returning to academia, I used to work on uncertainty quantification and free energy perturbation with my beloved MLR team at Bayer in Berlin.

I did a short postdoc at Aalto University with Vikas Garg and Markus Heinonen. I was incredibly lucky to do my PhD with Søren Hauberg and Georgios Arvanitidis, on differential geometry in machine learning. You can read my thesis [here](https://orbit.dtu.dk/en/publications/differential-geometric-approaches-to-machine-learning/).

I tend to be a serial-workshop organizer with GRaM [2024](https://gram-workshop.github.io/2024/), TAG-DS [2025](https://www.tagds.com/events/tag-ds-2025), GRaM [2026](https://gram-workshop.github.io/index.html), NeurReps [2026](https://www.neurreps.org/).

Oh, and I am a _huge_ information-geometry nerd. I fell down that rabbit hole after reading [Frank Nielsen's work](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.08271) back in 2018 and never really climbed back out. If you somehow ended up here, and feel like nerding out, please say hi! I am based in Oxford, and sometimes in London, Berlin, Nantes, Copenhagen, Amsterdam and Vienna.
